The Programme — Training Sites

Ten training sites across eight organisations.

Across sixty months a typical resident rotates through [number] of the ten sites — figure not yet released by the Programme Office.

How many of the ten a given resident sees depends on the exit pathway taken, on the advanced rotations chosen in senior residency, and on which postings are open in a given year. Senior residency requires advanced rotations at two or more sites in different organisations, so the floor is set; the typical figure is not published, and this page will not invent one.

No other residency in Singapore trains its residents inside eight different employers.

Duration, the two exit pathways and the list of participating organisations are as published by NUHS. The comparison against other Singapore residencies is the programme’s own, drawn from published rotation lists. Confirm the comparison before publication.

The eight participating organisations

Two ministries beyond Health, a statutory board, a uniformed service and all three healthcare clusters. Ten rotation sites sit across them.

Participating organisations in the National Preventive Medicine Residency Programme: the sector each sits in, the stage of the sixty months a resident is typically posted there, and the work itself.
Organisation Sector Typical year What you do there
Ministry of Health Ministry Years 2–3, junior residency Work on the policy, financing and regulatory side of the system — subsidy and provider regulation, and national programmes such as the childhood immunisation schedule.
Health Promotion Board Statutory board Years 1–2, junior residency Design and run population health programmes — screening uptake, behavioural intervention, and food and beverage measures such as Nutri-Grade labelling.
Ministry of Manpower Ministry Years 3–4 Set and enforce workplace exposure standards, including the permissible noise exposure limits that decide how long a worker may stay on a floor.
Ministry of Home Affairs Ministry Years 4–5, senior residency Deliver health services across the Home Team — police, civil defence, immigration and prisons — where the population you are responsible for is the workforce itself.
Singapore Armed Forces Uniformed service Years 4–5, senior residency Practise military and occupational medicine: medical fitness standards, heat injury and training load, and force health protection for a largely conscript population.
National Healthcare Group Healthcare cluster Years 1–2, junior residency Take population health at cluster scale — responsibility for a defined resident population in the central region, not only for the patients who present.
SingHealth Healthcare cluster Years 2–3, junior residency The same cluster-scale population health work against a different population mix, a different provider network and a different set of constraints.
National University Health System Healthcare cluster, and Sponsoring Institution Year 1, and again in senior residency Work inside an academic health system — teaching, research supervision, and the health services research that turns a routine dataset into a published evaluation.

What rotating this widely actually gives you

Breadth is only worth having if it changes what you can do. Here is what it changes.

Both sides of the table

You write an exposure standard at the Ministry of Manpower, then you are the one applying it in a cluster or in the Armed Forces. Very few people in Singapore public health have sat on both sides of that document, and the ones who have are the ones asked to redraft it.

The same population, counted twice

National Healthcare Group and SingHealth do the same job for different populations. Working in both is the cheapest way to learn which findings are a real effect and which are an artefact of a particular catchment.

Instruments, not opinions

Nutri-Grade beverage labelling, the childhood immunisation schedule, permissible noise exposure limits, hawker centre hygiene grading. Each of these is an instrument somebody had to draft, defend against objection, and then evaluate. Rotating this widely means you watch that happen from the inside more than once.

A network across government by the time you finish

The posting ends; the colleagues do not. A resident who has worked inside two ministries, a statutory board, a uniformed service and two clusters finishes the programme knowing, by name, the people who will be their counterparts for the next thirty years. That is not a soft benefit — it is the difference between a proposal that moves and one that circulates.

Descriptions of the training model, not claims about any individual resident’s rotations. The senior residency requirement for advanced rotations at two or more sites in different organisations is as specified by the programme; everything above it varies by pathway and by year.
