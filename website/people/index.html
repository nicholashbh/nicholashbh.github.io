Who teaches you, and who trains beside you.

Core faculty

Teaching is not held in one department: the programme draws its faculty from across the eight participating organisations, so the person supervising a rotation is usually the person who runs the work.

That is the practical difference between this residency and a departmental one. A resident on an occupational health rotation is supervised by someone who sets exposure standards for a living; a resident on a policy rotation is supervised by someone who writes policy. The roster below is published in full, with titles and employing organisation, so an applicant can see who that would be before applying rather than after matching.

Current residents

Five intakes are in training at any one time, because the programme runs for sixty months and takes one intake a year, each July.

Cohort size is published as a number rather than as a grid of portraits, because a portrait grid can be padded with faces from other programmes and a number cannot.

Ask a current resident

The section that matters most on this page, and the one that is still unfinished.

Writing to a Programme Director from a work account puts your name on documented interest in leaving your department. That is a real cost, and it lands hardest on the people with the least room to absorb it — a trainee mid-rotation, or anyone whose department head also signs their references.

So two residents have agreed to take questions from prospective applicants, with the Programme Office not copied in. What you ask them does not come back to us, and it does not appear anywhere in your application. Ask them the things this site cannot answer honestly about itself: what the sixty months actually feel like, what the MPH year costs you in evenings, and whether they would choose it again.
