What the sponsored MPH obliges you to

The Master of Public Health is fully sponsored. A sponsorship is an obligation, so here is the obligation in full.

You will complete the Master of Public Health at the NUS Saw Swee Hock School of Public Health during junior residency, and you will not pay for it. What follows is what accepting that costs you, stated before you apply rather than at the point of signing.

Programme fee sponsored
S$48,000 — the full cost of the part-time Master of Public Health at NUS Saw Swee Hock School of Public Health, including examination fees.
Who pays
The Sponsoring Institution, through MOH Holdings.
Additional service obligation
Two years, served after specialist accreditation.
Against an existing bond
Runs consecutively to an existing MOH or PSC bond, not concurrently. Your total obligation is the sum of the two.
If you leave early
Liquidated damages are pro-rated against the unserved portion of the obligation.
If you leave mid-residency
Leaving during residency ends the sponsorship from that date; fees already paid are not recovered.

Source: the sponsorship instrument itself, not a summary of it. Verified [date] by [Finance or Sponsoring Institution contact, email]. Whether the additional obligation runs consecutively or concurrently to an existing bond is the single highest-consequence line on this page. Ask for it in writing, from the Sponsoring Institution, before you sign anything.

Who can apply

Three routes in, set out in the same order, so you can find your own line without reading all three.

Singapore Medical Officer or House Officer

Eligibility
Doctors registered with the Singapore Medical Council. Confirm the registration status required at the point of entry, and whether any minimum period of post-registration clinical experience applies.
What you submit
Interview registration with the Sponsoring Institution first, then the application itself through the MOH Holdings eResidency portal. Confirm the full document list: referee reports, transcripts, personal statement, portfolio.
Key dates
The cycle above applies in full. Interview registration closes 30 September 2026; the eResidency portal closes 20 November 2026.

Medical student

Eligibility
Confirm whether final-year medical students may apply directly to this programme, or must first complete Postgraduate Year 1. Either way, entry is through the same MOH Holdings cycle as everybody else.
What you submit
The same interview registration and eResidency application. Confirm what stands in for referee reports and service records where a student has neither.
Key dates
The same two deadlines: 30 September 2026 and 20 November 2026. If you are not yet eligible this cycle, the useful thing to do now is attend the information session and ask.

Overseas applicant

Eligibility
Doctors trained outside Singapore apply through the same eResidency cycle. Conditional registration with the Singapore Medical Council: confirm whether an international medical graduate must already hold it at the point of application, or only by the start of the intake. This is genuinely ambiguous and must not be guessed here.
What you submit
The same interview registration and eResidency application. Confirm what registration and good-standing evidence the primary registering authority must supply, and in what form.
Key dates
The same two deadlines: 30 September 2026 and 20 November 2026. Allow additional time before them for Singapore Medical Council registration; confirm the current processing time.

How you are selected

The criteria are published, and they are the same criteria the interview is built around. There is nothing else being scored that we are not telling you about.

Selection criteria
Clinical competence; interpersonal and communication skills; practice-based learning; professionalism; and personal attributes aligned with the NUHS TRICEP values.
Optional criteria
Quality of portfolio, and performance at interview.
Interview format
Four stations of about ten minutes each, with two minutes of reading time before the stations that use case scenarios.
How the criteria are weighted
Confirm whether the criteria carry different weights, and whether the optional criteria can change an outcome on their own.

Selection criteria as published by the programme. Interview format is provisional and must be confirmed with the Sponsoring Institution before this page is published. Verified [date] by [named person, email].

What you earn during training

Nothing about choosing this programme changes your pay as a resident. What it changes is what comes after.

Residency pay in Singapore follows flat national MOH Holdings terms. It is set by year of training, not by specialty, so a preventive medicine resident and a resident in any other programme at the same stage are paid on the same scale. There is no premium for this programme and no penalty for it.

During training

National MOH Holdings residency terms, by year of training. The sponsored Master of Public Health sits on top of that, and is not paid out to you in cash — it is a fee the Sponsoring Institution pays on your behalf, with the obligation set out above.

After accreditation

Where this leads financially depends on which of the eight participating organisations you join, and on whether you take a ministry, statutory board or cluster post. Add the actual figures: the current MOHH residency pay scale by year, and the published salary bands for the destination posts graduates typically enter. Until those are in place, this page will not put a number here.

Weigh this honestly: the earnings profile of a preventive medicine physician is different from that of a hospital specialist with access to private practice, and for most people it is lower over a career. What you are buying instead is reach — decisions that apply to a whole population rather than to the patient in front of you.

Residency pay is set nationally by MOH Holdings and is outside the programme's control. No pay figure is published on this page until it can be cited to a current MOH Holdings source. Verified [date] by [named person, email].

If you have missed the interview registration window

This is the deadline applicants miss, because it is not the one on the portal.

Interview registration with the Sponsoring Institution closes almost two months before the eResidency portal, so it is entirely possible to be watching the portal date and to have already missed the one that mattered. If it has closed, write to the Programme Office and ask what is still open to you this cycle — one email costs less than a year of assuming the answer. If the answer is that you have to wait, the next intake is [confirm the next cycle and its dates], and the intervening year is better spent on a public health or occupational health project you can speak to at interview than on waiting quietly.
