How to apply, and what it commits you to.

The cycle, the three routes in, and the criteria you are assessed against.

Current cycle — July 2027 intake. The eResidency portal opens 1 September 2026 and closes 24 September 2026.

Read the five-year structure before you apply

The cycle

Seven dates. The eResidency portal closes on 24 September and will not be reopened or extended.

Two things run at once and people conflate them. The application is submitted to MOH Holdings through the eResidency portal. Registering to be interviewed is done with NUHS as Sponsoring Institution, separately, and stays open eight days after the portal has shut. It is the portal date that is fatal.

Application cycle for the July 2027 intake, as published in MOH Holdings' Junior Residency application guide. The eResidency application and interview registration are separate steps with different closing dates.
Stage When What you do
eResidency portal 1 to 24 September 2026 Submit the application and portfolio through the MOH Holdings portal, naming Preventive Medicine as a programme choice. Pay the S$100 application fee by credit card — the submission is not complete without it, and it is not refundable. The window will not be reopened or extended.
Referees submit 1 September to 2 October 2026 Up to four referees. They are only notified once you click submit, and their details cannot be edited afterwards, so get them right the first time.
Interview registration with NUHS 1 September to 2 October 2026 Register with NUHS as Sponsoring Institution to be interviewed. This is separate from the portal and does not happen through it.
Interviews 19 October to 30 November 2026 Sponsoring Institutions organise and conduct the interviews. Since the 2021 intake these are run by the individual programmes rather than centrally by MOH Holdings.
Ranking 5 to 8 January 2027 Submit your ranked preferences, up to six programmes. Your ranking is confidential and is not shown to Programme Directors. Withdrawing from this point on forfeits your place in the next exercise.
Match and scramble 18 January to 12 February 2027 MOH Holdings runs the national match against the Sponsoring Institutions' own rankings.
Results Early March 2027 Match results are released by MOH Holdings. Training begins on the first working day of July 2027.

Dates from MOH Holdings' Junior Residency application guide for the July 2027 intake, published August 2026. Where MOH Holdings and this page disagree, MOH Holdings governs. Dates are reviewed each August when the guide for the following cycle is published.

Who can apply

Three routes in, and the four conditions that apply whichever one you come by.

Four conditions apply to everybody, whatever route you come by. You must be an employee of MOH Holdings at the time of application. You must hold a local medical degree, or a primary medical qualification registrable under the Second Schedule of the Medical Registration Act. You must have completed Postgraduate Year 1 and hold valid Conditional or Full Registration with the Singapore Medical Council. And you must be at least Postgraduate Year 2 by the July you would enter.

Everyone starts at Residency Year 1. MOH Holdings is explicit that prior experience does not buy advanced standing, and that no pre-selection or off-cycle start is allowed.

Singapore Medical Officer or House Officer

Eligibility
The four conditions above. Your Medical Officer years do not shorten the programme and are not credited as advanced standing — but they are what makes you credible at interview.
What you submit
A portfolio through the eResidency portal, the S$100 fee, and up to four referees. A referee must have supervised or worked with you for at least three weeks, and must be a medical school faculty member, an appointed clinical tutor, or at least a hospital registrar or lecturer.
Key dates
The cycle above applies in full. Portal 1 to 24 September 2026; interview registration to 2 October 2026.

Medical student

Eligibility
You cannot enter this programme straight from medical school. Postgraduate Year 1 must be completed, and you must be at least Postgraduate Year 2 by the July you would start — so the earliest realistic application is during your first postgraduate year, for entry the year after.
What you submit
The same portfolio and referees. MOH Holdings weighs academic scores and Student Internship Programme performance alongside interview performance and letters of reference, so the student record does carry into the application.
Key dates
The same cycle. If you are not yet eligible, the useful thing to do now is write to the Programme Director and ask what a competitive application looks like a year out.

Overseas applicant

Eligibility
The order matters and it catches people out. MOH Holdings states that graduates from overseas must first secure an offer of employment as a doctor from MOH Holdings before they are eligible to apply for residency at all. Employment comes first, application second. Placement is prioritised for Singapore citizens, Singapore permanent residents and locally-trained foreigners.
Mid-stream entry
The Specialists Accreditation Board allows international medical graduates to enter part-way through, with the latest point of entry being Year 1 of senior residency. In practice this route is exercised rarely and only on the Residency Advisory Committee's assessment of documented equivalent training; passing postgraduate examinations is not accepted as equivalent on its own. Applicants coming through the ordinary MOH Holdings match enter at Residency Year 1.
What you submit
The same portfolio and referees. MOH Holdings advises Singaporean international medical graduates to return to Singapore as soon as they complete their studies, and takes enquiries at physician@mohh.com.sg.

Eligibility from MOH Holdings' published residency FAQs and its July 2027 application guide, and from the Specialists Accreditation Board's Preventive Medicine Specialty Training Requirements. Eligibility is set by MOH Holdings and the Singapore Medical Council, not by the programme. Where their published requirements and this page differ, theirs govern.

Two further disqualifiers are published and are easy to miss. A doctor involuntarily separated from residency or specialist training previously is not eligible to reapply. An applicant who is undergoing, or has undergone, any disciplinary proceeding, inquiry or investigation must declare it to MOH Holdings and may be de-prioritised in the match.

How you are selected

The published criteria, and the interview at which they are assessed.

Programme criteria, base
Five, published by NUHS: clinical competence; interpersonal and communication skills; practice-based learning and improvement; professionalism; and personal attributes aligned with the NUHS TRICEP values — teamwork, respect, integrity, compassion, excellence and patient-centredness.
Programme criteria, optional
Two: the quality of your portfolio, including resumes, personal statements and reference letters; and your performance at interview. NUHS states plainly that meeting the criteria makes you eligible but does not guarantee a match.
National criteria
MOH Holdings applies its own set across all programmes: academic scores, Student Internship Programme performance, interview performance, and letters of reference. Sponsoring Institutions may add criteria of their own on top.
Interview format
Run by the programme, not centrally. Since the 2021 intake, the Multiple Mini Interview previously conducted by MOH Holdings has been replaced by interviews held by each programme within its Sponsoring Institution. NUHS Preventive Medicine interviews as a panel: one sitting of about forty minutes with the Programme Director and two core faculty, covering your portfolio, your reasons for the specialty, and one public health scenario given to you at the start of the interview.
How the criteria are weighted
No published weighting is applied. The panel scores each base criterion independently and the two optional criteria — portfolio quality and interview performance — are considered alongside them rather than as a tie-break. A strong interview does not on its own carry an application that fails a base criterion.

Programme criteria as published on the NUHS programme page; national criteria and the interview arrangement from MOH Holdings.

If you have missed a deadline

Which deadline you missed decides whether you still have a cycle.

If you missed the portal, you have missed the cycle. MOH Holdings states that the application window will not be reopened or extended, and there is no route around that. The next intake is July 2028, and the portal for it opens around September 2027 on the same pattern. Confirm the exact dates against MOH Holdings when the guide for that cycle is published.

If you submitted but missed interview registration, that is a different and more recoverable problem. MOH Holdings' own guide tells late applicants to email the programmes directly to check whether slots remain. Do that rather than assuming.

If you are out of this cycle, the intervening year is better spent on a public health or occupational health project you can speak to at interview than on waiting quietly. Write to the Programme Office and ask what would make an application stronger next time.
