The national residency in Preventive Medicine.

  • 5 years Full-time to specialist accreditation
  • Built-in MPH, local or overseas* Opportunity for sponsorship to study part-time at NUS Saw Swee Hock School of Public Health†
  • Beyond the Hospital Train in ministries, statutory boards, all three healthcare clusters and more
  • 2 exit accreditation pathways Public Health, or Occupational Medicine

* Sponsorship is subject to approval by the Residency Advisory Committee, including approval of an overseas school.

† MPH sponsorship carries a one-year bond with a public healthcare institution, served concurrently with any existing bond. Terms and conditions apply.

Beyond the hospital

Train in ministries, statutory boards, all three healthcare clusters and more. Below are some examples:

  • Ministry of HealthDivisions across health policy, financing, regulations and non communicable diseases
  • Health Promotion BoardPopulation health programmes and behavioural intervention
  • Communicable Diseases AgencyDivisions across disease surveillance, outbreak preparedness, and foodborne and vector-borne disease
  • Ministry of ManpowerThe Occupational Safety and Health Division, and national workplace exposure standards
  • Ministry of Home AffairsHome Team Medical Services; police, civil defence, immigration and prisons
  • Singapore Armed ForcesThe SAF Medical Corps: military and occupational medicine
  • National Healthcare GroupPopulation health for central Singapore, and the National Centre for Infectious Diseases
  • SingHealthPopulation health for eastern Singapore, and the Office of Regional Health
  • National University Health SystemPopulation health for western Singapore, and the academic health system

Examples, not a full list and not a promise of placement. Rotations are tailored from the second year onwards: which organisations a resident sees depends on the exit pathway and on the rotations chosen with the Programme Director.

Dear colleague,

With the challenges facing Singapore, Preventive Medicine physicians are needed as never before. Singapore faces a convergence of an ageing population, increasing chronic disease, escalating healthcare costs and a maelstrom of healthcare reforms at macro, meso and micro levels. These are bread-and-butter issues for the Preventive Medicine practitioner.

If you are weighing this against a hospital specialty, weigh it honestly. You will spend most of your five years outside a ward, your work will rarely have a face attached to it, and the results arrive years after the decision. In exchange you get reach that clinical practice cannot offer, and problems that do not resolve at discharge.

Whichever way you decide, decide it with the full picture. The Programme Office and two current residents will answer anything this site does not.

A/Prof Jason Yap Programme Director, National Preventive Medicine Residency Programme
Opening paragraph quoted from an interview with the Singapore Medical Association, 2018.
